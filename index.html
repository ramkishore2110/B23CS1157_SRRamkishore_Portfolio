About

Security first, then everything else.

I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at Mar Baselios College of Engineering and Technology, Trivandrum. Most of what I enjoy sits at the seam between cryptography and applied AI — how you scramble an image so convincingly that a generative model has nothing left to morph, and how you make a language model useful without letting it wander.

At ADE, DRDO I spent a winter reading other people’s aerospace code for a living — secure code characterization against ADA compliance rules. Before that, ICT Academy of Kerala put me on network monitoring and vulnerability analysis with Kali, Wireshark and Burp. In between, IBM SkillsBuild trained me to build agentic AI workflows in Python.

Outside coursework I coordinate the NSS RUDHIRASENA unit’s blood donation drives and hold Level 5 in the µLearn community with 6,470+ karma points.
